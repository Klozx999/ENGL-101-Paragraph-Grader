How the grade is calculated

Five criteria are each scored out of 10 using the ENGL 101 Paragraph Writing Rubric (total out of 50). The mark that goes into the gradebook uses the same formula as the WP1 sheet:

=SUM(D12:H12)/50*7.5 → Total WP1 (out of 7.5)
